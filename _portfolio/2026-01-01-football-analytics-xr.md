This research explored two complementary applications of emerging technology to American football: computer vision-based player tracking and XR-based game visualization.

On the computer vision side, the work involved developing pipelines for pose estimation and skeletal tracking of football players from video footage, translating raw visual data into structured spatial data representing player positions and movements in field coordinates. This included work with Grounded SAM 2 and related tools for player detection and segmentation.

On the XR side, the research explored how augmented and virtual reality could be used to present football analytics data in ways that reveal hidden game structure — formation identification, coverage assignments, route patterns, and spatial relationships between players — in formats that go beyond traditional 2D film review.

**Stack:** Python, PyTorch, Unity, RealityKit, SwiftUI, Unreal Engine
